Math Fasters

Look and sound ideas. Pick what your kids would like.

The game works today with a plain look. Everything below is optional polish. Nothing here is built into the game yet. Anything we add will come with a switch in grown-up settings, so the current plain look stays available for older tablets.

How to answer: every idea has a code like 2B. Reply with the codes you want, for example "go with 1A, 2B and 3B". Ideas marked now show how the game looks or sounds today.

1Characters

A friendly face that reacts to each answer. Pick one. All three are original designs made for this game.

1ATock the co-pilot robot

A small hovering robot. Its screen face and antenna light show how it feels.

Shows up in the corner of the play screen and on the results screen.

1BZuzu the space pet

A squishy alien pet with glowing feelers. It bounces when you get one right.

Rides along on the play screen and waits at the next planet on the mission map.

1CCadet, the kid astronaut

A kid in a space suit. Each pilot could pick their own suit color, hair and skin.

Shows up on the pilot picker, the results screen and the mission map.

2Space and asteroids

Three asteroid looks. The numbers stay big, white and outlined in all of them.

2AFlat rocks now

Simple grey shapes. Clear, but plain.

2BRocky asteroids

Lit from one side, with craters and a bright edge. A dark patch sits behind each number.

2CGlowing crystals

Icy crystals with a soft glow. Could also be used only for bonus rounds.

Two backgrounds. The asteroids are the same in both so you can compare.

2DPlain starfield now

Dark sky with still stars.

2EDeep space

Soft color clouds, stars that drift at three speeds, and a far-off planet. Kept dim so numbers stay easy to read.

3Sounds

Tap a button to hear each sound. Turn your volume up a little first. No sound plays until you tap.

3ASimple beeps now

Plain buzzes and a burst of hiss.

3BRicher sounds

Fuller laser, a boom with a low rumble, a bright chime, and a gentle "bonk" for mistakes.

3CBackground tune

A short looping tune that plays quietly under the game. It would get its own on/off switch in Settings.

4Extra juice

Small touches that make right answers feel great.

4AStreak flames

A meter fills as you get answers right in a row. The flames grow and a score bonus appears. One miss resets it.

4CChunky explosions

Left is today. Right adds a flash, a ring, and rock chunks that spin away.
